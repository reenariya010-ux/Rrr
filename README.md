# Rrr
For Nivaan Singhaniya, life is strictly about rules, discipline, and perfection. To him, love is nothing but a total waste of time. Tisha, on the other hand, is a carefree, endlessly chatty girl who spends her days dreaming of her 'Dream Prince.' What happens when these two opposite worlds collide? Will Nivaan's rigid rules break before Tisha’?
